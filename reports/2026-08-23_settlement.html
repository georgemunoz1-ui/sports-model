The fear was that a prior-heavy challenger buys calibration by sacrificing discrimination. It did the opposite. Discrimination went up — AUC 0.660 against the champion’s 0.580 and the baseline’s 0.500 — and calibration is the half that failed: a slope of 5.86 means the outcomes moved about six times as much as the forecasts did. The challenger has ordering skill its probabilities do not express, which is a different and more fixable defect than the one the compression statistic suggested.

The champion’s Brier skill over the prior is +0.00076. That is three orders of magnitude below the challenger’s and is indistinguishable from zero at any sample size: on this slate the frozen V13 arm added essentially nothing over one league-average number.

Both fitted arms pass the Hessian estimability rule and fail acceptability separately, which is exactly the separation that rule exists to keep.

The champion went 4–0 with a perfect AUC — on four games it was action-blocked for, its feature state 32 days stale against a 21-day ceiling. Four Bernoulli trials cannot separate a good model from a lucky one; a perfect AUC on n=4 happens about 4% of the time under a coin flip. The honest reading is “not evaluable and interesting”, not that the stale arm was right all along. The challenger returns byte-identical numbers to the baseline, because on this sport it is the baseline — which is what a negative control is supposed to look like.

Both spellings of one franchise appeared in a single day: the relay wrote POR and LV, the card wrote PDX and LVA. Bound through one canonical id. Before yesterday’s amendment, two of these four games would have failed to pair silently.

What this changes, and what it does not

  • The challenger needs its offset widened, not re-regularised. A slope near 6 says the shrinkage is far too aggressive for the signal that is actually there.
  • The total arms need to beat a league-mean number before any of them is worth reporting. Right now none does.
  • Retaining the simulated total distribution is now the binding blocker on log score and CRPS. It has to be wired before the next card.
  • Nothing here moves the promotion gate, the ledger, or the verdict. One night is one cluster.

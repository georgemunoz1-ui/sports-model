0 · Design doctrine (the spine everything hangs on)

The five-step hierarchy (locked after your objective-function correction):

  • 1. Accurate calibrated probabilities — DATA > Implied. Judged vs outcomes (win%, Brier, MAE, calibration), never vs the market's opinion.
  • 2. Correct objective — a bet is judged by EV vs the price's break-even, not win% > 50%. A 45% dog at +150 is a win for the strategy.
  • 3. Decision vs price — act only when model p beats break-even by a ≥4pp uncertainty buffer.
  • 4. Risk control — half-weight thin samples (<10 GS), void no-starts, blowout-risk guardrail, R5 rule ("edge > 35% = probably your own bug — audit first").
  • 5. Outcome tracking — pre-registered plays only enter the ledger; retro-casts are labeled and excluded; verdicts move only at gate sample sizes.

2 · Engine A — K-prop projector LIVE PAPER

The track that earns plays. Per starter, using starts only (relief outings excluded — a caught bug):

K-rate = (ΣSO + 0.22·80) / (ΣTBF + 80) ← shrunk toward league 22% with an 80-TBF prior E[TBF] = (ΣTBF + 24·3) / (starts + 3) ← expected workload, shrunk toward 24 batters E[K] = K-rate × E[TBF] P(K ≥ line) = Poisson tail at λ = E[K] ← closed-form (zero simulation noise)

Play rule: best price across the books on your board → EV = p·profit − (1−p) → PLAY iff EV > 0 AND edge ≥ 4pp AND |E[K] − line| ≥ 0.8. Half-weight if < 10 GS. Skip if < 5 GS. Void if pitcher doesn't start.

Validation (n = 1,759 historical starts): MAE 1.92 K vs naive last-start 2.41 vs league-avg 1.99; P(over) calibration buckets track (model 60% → overs hit 58%); displaced lines punished at 62-70% vs 52.4% break-even.

Forward ledger: day 1 = 1-2-1V, −0.47u. Day 2 pending tonight (Cole U6.5, Singer U5.5 @FD, Jump O4.5 @+110, Bennett U5.5 ½). Gate: 100 graded plays, CI-positive ROI, your sign-off — then and only then real money.

Known gaps (= experiment queue): no opponent-K% adjustment (E1, next), Poisson may under-fatten tails (E2), no pitch-count/opener intel (mitigated by the starter-confirmation rule).

3 · Engine B — V13.1 game simulator TRACKING ONLY

Monte Carlo (30,000 sims/game) producing win%, projected total, run-line cover, and blowout risk. Displayed on every card per your standing order; closed for betting.

SP run-suppression = shrunk_ERA − ((K% − BB%) − 13.5)·0.03 where shrunk_ERA = w·ERA + (1−w)·4.20, w = GS/(GS+8) Team offense = RF/g ÷ 4.45 (league) · bullpen = 0.55·team RA + 0.45·league (double-count fix) Park effect scales with opposing pitcher's ball-in-play rate · HFA = 1.005 (fit from base rates) Runs ~ Poisson-Gamma; variance shape driven by opposing K% (contact arms → fat tails) Outputs: P(home win) · E[total] · P(cover ±1.5) · P(lose by ≥5) per side

The verdict that closed it (n = 1,461 as-of games): directional accuracy 53.0% vs a 52% home base rate; Brier 0.2500 = exactly coin, vs market ~0.238. A gradient-boosted model on the same features did worse (49.7% / 0.2563) — the ceiling is the inputs, not the algorithm. Season rates + team form simply don't carry what the market prices (lineups, platoons, bullpen state, injuries).

What survived validation: the blowout-risk guardrail (≥18% flagged picks got blown out 33% vs 5% for the rest — 6.6× separation) and the reliability finding that V13's honest range is ~40-65% (its old "80%+ locks" won just 61% — which is why calibration crushes displayed confidence).

Reopen gate: a new feature class (lineup wOBA, bullpen rest) beating market Brier on a ≥500-game holdout. Until then: display, don't bet.

7 · Honest limitations (the list we work, not hide)

  • Sides are a coin flip on free season-level data — proven twice; only richer inputs change that.
  • K-prop edge is plausible but unproven forward — the backtest is real; whether flagged line-deviations are book error vs book information (opponent K%, pitch plans) is exactly what the 100-play gate answers.
  • Prop prices depend on your RotoWire drop (pages unscrapeable); no board → projection-only card.
  • No injury/lineup/weather feeds; rookie starters skip; totals module weak; the ecosystem (WNBA/NFL/NBA/CFB) is scaffolded, not populated.

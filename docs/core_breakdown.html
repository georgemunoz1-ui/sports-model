From observed data to an auditable result

  1. Capture and reconcile. Retain raw source bytes, event/team identities, retrieval and availability timestamps. Missing inputs stay explicit; acquisition of historical data today does not prove historical availability.
  2. Fit chronologically. Transformations, coefficients, residual calibration and selector choices use earlier permitted data. Preserve original controls and freeze protocols before confirmation.
  3. Execute the real runner. Shared cards use scripts/generate_card_force.py; NFL uses scripts/run_nfl_integrated.py; CFB/NBA challengers use scripts/run_efficiency_experiments.py and the explicit shared-card efficiency configuration.
  4. Retain component receipts. Bind event, cutoff, model/stage, fitted state, source hashes and numerical identity. Winner Bernoulli trials are not score draws; 100,000 draws are not 100,000 games.
  5. Price exact contracts and select separately. Independent models remain separate from decision-time prices. Explicit market-informed procedures have separate identities. Keep raw predictions when selection returns BLOCKED or NO_PICK.
  6. Archive, register and grade. Verify returned archive bytes, register genuine pre-start forecasts, acquire final results and apply the exact payout rules. A local log or empty grading preflight does not complete this chain.

Core-market arithmetic

M = home score - away score; T = home score + away score
Home win      = P(M > 0), with separately declared tie/OT treatment
Home cover    = P(M + home handicap > 0)
Push          = P(M + home handicap = 0)
Over line L   = P(T > L)
Net EV/unit   = p_win * (decimal_odds - 1) - p_loss

The EV expression assumes ordinary full-win/full-loss contracts with pushes refunded. Half outcomes, voids, exchange fees and house rules require explicit payouts. A margin law alone cannot identify totals. Related same-game legs must settle from compatible joint scenarios, not assumed-independent marginal probabilities.

What is wrong with this card

  1. 26 of 30 arms had already started. This is the third board in a row to arrive mid-slate. The single most valuable thing that could change is a capture before first pitch.
  2. n=12 on the shrinkage fit. The convergence with the 7/25 outcome slope is encouraging and is still two small samples agreeing, not a result.
  3. Neither the 7/25 outcome regression nor this market regression was pre-registered. Both are hypothesis-generating. E5 is now pre-registered against an untouched forward holdout and the engine has not been touched.
  4. The residual correlates with the line at 0.91. If E5 confirms shrinkage, then most of what this engine has been calling 'edge' was an artifact of buying low lines, and the 9 graded plays to date need re-reading in that light.
  5. The quarantine threshold of 1.0 K is a judgement call I made, not a fitted parameter. It excludes 10 rows. A different threshold gives a different slope.
  6. Two rows (Baz on BAL, Suarez on BOS) looked like source errors and were not. The pre-slate card confirmed the board. Worth recording because hand-correcting them would have been the mistake.
  7. Hard Rock's prices are outside the image. Six book columns were advertised; five are readable.
  8. Still no closing price has ever been captured, so nothing here can be checked against a close.

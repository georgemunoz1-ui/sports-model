6 · What the 100k run actually showed

“Pure noise” was too definitive and I have withdrawn it. The largest champion-vs-100k gap on this board is 0.7127pp, about 2.18σ against a Monte Carlo error near 0.29pp. Stated alone that sounds like a finding. It was the largest of ten comparisons, and P(at least one of 10 reaches 2.18σ) = 0.2569 — a one-in-four event, which is not evidence of anything. The honest label is CONSISTENT_WITH_MONTE_CARLO_VARIATION: it asserts compatibility, not identity. Proving the two runs are the same model requires a test I have not run.

7 · Hold policy v2 — two dimensions, versioned not backdated

My first same-book rule was an absolute 3.5–7.5% overround band, generalised from one observation of one market type. It would have rejected your valid board, whose MLB moneylines hold 1.637–1.869%. I replaced it with homogeneity within a market type — and that alone is still not sufficient, because a board quoting a uniform 20% hold on every game is perfectly homogeneous and would sail through a spread test. 8 · The total prior’s tail, and two means that are not the same number

The predictive pmf was supported on 0–40 and therefore assigned probability zero to a 41-run game. Under the exact-integer-mass log score that is an infinite penalty for an outcome the support simply refused to imagine. Baseball totals have no upper bound. The distribution now carries an explicit OVERFLOW_ABOVE_SUPPORT bin, so a 41+ game scores a large finite penalty rather than an infinite one.

EXPANDING_TOTAL_POINT_MEAN8.9911the arithmetic mean of 1684 observed totals through 2026-08-21
EXPANDING_TOTAL_PMF_MEAN8.9976the mean of the smoothed predictive pmf over its integer support, renormalized over the body
difference+0.0065unit pseudo-mass moves weight toward never-observed integers, most of which sit above the mode

They are different quantities and neither is a rounding of the other. Reporting one under the other’s name is the kind of substitution that survives review precisely because the numbers agree to two decimals. MLB totals remain NEVER_DECISION_BEARING; this is a scoring repair, not a route to action.

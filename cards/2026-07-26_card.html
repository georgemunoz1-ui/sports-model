What the totals column actually is — and why 12 of 15 totals are missing below

The source quotes the over and the under at different lines in 12 of 15 games, and the under line is never lower than the over line (displacement min +0.0, max +1.0, zero negatives). That signature is a best-price-across-books display, not one book's two-sided market: the best over sits at the lowest number on the board, the best under at the highest. Pairing them describes a middle, not a market.

So for those games the consensus total is not identified, only bounded — shown as [lo–hi]. No total lean is emitted for them. Only ATL@BAL, COL@MIL, HOU@CWS quote a single number on both sides and are treated as real. This is the same defect class as the 7/25 strikeout alt-line ladder, caught by the same displacement test. The moneyline column is unaffected — one price per team, unambiguous side attribution — which is why the pre-registered ledger, which trades moneylines only, is not contaminated by this.

Data integrity — what is wrong with this card

  1. Every price here was read from an image by a model. The audits above (15/15 schedule match, 26/30 starters, no-arb hold band) make transcription error unlikely, but they do not make it impossible. A CSV export from the source would remove this entire class of risk.
  2. 12 of 15 totals are not identified, only bounded, because the source displays best-price rather than a market. Any "total lean" for those games would be fabricated, so none is shown.
  3. The run-line column was not transcribed at all. Side attribution across the two rows per game is unverified, and under R3 sides are dead, so it was left out rather than guessed.
  4. ATH@MIN has a starter conflict — the board says Travis Adams, statsapi says Joe Ryan. The game was modeled with Joe Ryan. No side there cleared the rule, so nothing was registered either way, but the book's price may have been made against a different pitcher entirely.
  5. Rule 3's written text and its implementation disagree. The text says "TBD or <5 GS"; the code says "TBD or <2 GS". 7/25 was graded under the code's definition, so 7/26 was selected the same way for consistency. Checked explicitly: zero picks on this slate flip between the two readings. The text will be amended to match the code effective 7/27 — forward only, never re-selecting rows whose outcomes are already visible.
  6. No closing snapshot has ever been captured. Every price in this ledger is an opening or near-opening number, so closing-line value — the readout that actually settles whether this works — is still unmeasured. This is the binding constraint on the whole project, and it blocks experiment E4.
  7. V13 may simply be a favorite-fader. Across 50 priced, uncapped game-rows on 5 cards, V13's home win probability sits 0.086 below the market where the home team is a big favorite, and 0.019 below in the middle. Four of the five picks this ledger has ever made are underdogs. The ledger may be one systematic bet wearing several uniforms. This is a hypothesis generated from the same rows the ledger selects from, so it cannot confirm itself — it is pre-registered as E4 against a forward holdout from 7/27, and it was deliberately not used to touch today's picks.
  8. Still zero strikeout-prop prices, a third consecutive slate. The 100-play authorization gate remains stuck at 9/100. Model quality is not the bottleneck; price access is.

# A fixture that stays green when you delete the rule it guards is decorative — prove it kills before trusting it.
Rule: for every rule a module claims to enforce, mutate the rule away in a copy and confirm the selftest goes RED. A suite of presence-only assertions ("field is not None") proves the field exists, not that its value is right. Pin VALUES, not shapes.
Incident: 2026-08-22. Five roadmap modules were built in parallel, each passing its own selftest. An adversarial mutation pass found critical/high defects in all five and dozens of decorative fixtures — e.g. daily_baselines pinned no Brier or log-loss VALUE at all (the scoring arithmetic was entirely untested while the suite passed green), and starter_attribution reported a structural zero as a measurement. Had these been wired into healthcheck on the strength of their passing selftests, they would have manufactured false confidence in exactly the numbers they exist to police.
Corollary: building fast in parallel is fine; TRUSTING fast is not. The modules landed quarantined and unwired, and a green selftest was explicitly declared insufficient evidence to wire them.

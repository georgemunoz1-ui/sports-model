  • MIL@NYM — the prior opposed all three model arms. Milwaukee won 8–2: the slate’s primary diagnostic went to the models, and it was the only strong signal on the board.
  • KC@TOR and LAD@ATL — the two reselection crossovers. Both lost (13–2 and 1–0), and in both cases the frozen champion’s original side would have won. This is the night’s central finding: reselection at 50.1–50.6% moved off winners.
  • AZ@SF — champion and widened took Arizona, adaptive and prior took San Francisco. San Francisco won 6–1: the two arms that deferred to the baseline were right.

Pattern worth watching, not concluding from: on four of the five split games, the lower-conviction arm closer to the baseline was correct. One slate cannot establish that; it is registered as a question for the accumulation ledger.

Grading incident — this card’s first results version was wrong

What happened. The first end-of-night grading of this slate joined five of these games to the previous day’s finals of the same matchups — BAL@STL, HOU@NYY, KC@TOR, MIL@NYM and LAD@ATL are all continuing series, and the join was keyed on team names with no date or game-id requirement. It reported the active path at 5–1 when it was 2–5, the crossovers as a 1–1 split when they were 0–2, and a positive counterfactual P&L that was actually negative.

Why the id alone would not have saved it. Consecutive-series game ids are adjacent integers — KC@TOR is 822772 on 8/26 and 822771 on 8/27; HOU@NYY 823506 and 823503; LAD@ATL 824878 and 824879. A single-digit slip lands on the neighbouring day’s game with every team name still agreeing.

What now prevents it. A registered result-binding gate (harness/result_binding_gate.py) requires sport + game id + scheduled date + away + home on every result row, and refuses date mismatches, prior-date series joins, unmatched or ambiguous ids, finals timestamped before first pitch, and any aggregate touching a refused row — no partial credit, because the damage was never the five rows, it was the records, Brier scores and P&L computed over them. Its primary fixture feeds these exact five prior-day finals into this exact board and requires all five to be refused by name. Three mutation tests confirm the fixture cannot pass with the protections removed. The superseded record is retained unedited and accrues nothing.

# All-sports capability and anti-stagnation audit — 2026-10-23

This audit finds a usable research platform with substantial unfinished model and evaluation work. The immediate priority is truthful input state, exact forecast/settlement identity, and an enforceable validation boundary. It is not a clean-sheet rewrite. Existing fitted models, residual laws, source receipts and retrospective evaluations should be reused with their limitations intact.

**Scope:** pinned main `90daf8e0036c67a5304aa0747bf50b2266e0b364` in `georgemunoz1-ui/sports-model`, plus read-only inspection of open PRs 52, 53, 68–71 and 73–75. PRs are not merged main; their test/evaluation counts below are reported in pinned PR bodies, not newly rerun by this audit. The local repository was restored through the connector during this task. Missing local data archives do not establish that the canonical repository is broken. This document neither certifies production nor changes weights, defaults, historical forecasts, ledgers or wagering authority.

The October 21–22 anecdotes are discovery exhibits. The causal explanations, roster/player effect sizes, and calibration conclusions in the conversation are not established by those games. Reported results also require exact-version, final-result identity reconciliation before evaluation.

## Stage vocabulary

Each capability has independent stages: **implemented** (callable source exists), **fitted** (identified parameters exist), **evaluated** (identified target/population/split results exist), **registered** (a policy refers to that identity and evidence), and **active** (the actual selected path consumed it for a particular event). Passing unit tests establishes behavior under fixtures, not predictive quality. Fresh source capture establishes retrieval time, not a fresh state or a numerical contribution. A default name establishes selection policy, not calibration. This is a source audit; no new historical fit was executed here.

This audit does **not** recommend removing every cap, refusing every unvalidated raw forecast, or automatically shipping every repair. Domain bounds and numerical-stability clips are different from empirical shrinkage choices. A repair changes verified implementation semantics and needs localized regression tests; a new predictive procedure needs chronological evaluation. Changed semantics still get a new identity even when labeled a repair.

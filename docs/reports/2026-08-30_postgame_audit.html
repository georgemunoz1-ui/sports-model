What this report is not

It is not a list of winning and losing picks. A pick that won over an unbound cohort tells you nothing about the model, and this window produced a great many of those. The organizing question is narrower and harsher: for each result, can the system prove which game it belongs to? Where it can, the result is evidence. Where it cannot, the result may still be true and may still be displayed — it simply accrues nothing.

Every counter below was produced by harness/result_binding_gate.binding_proof running over the frozen boards in this pass. None were typed. Where a figure comes from the audit run rather than this pass, it says so.

What actually blocks MLB

Re-running the post-slate proof for 8/28 returns 0/0 on every counter — nothing claimed, because there are zero result rows to bind. The pitcher archive's 8/28 file carries 930 rows across 61 columns and score_cols=[]. It has never carried a runs column; the standing finding sits at 0/126.

So MLB's blocker is not identity. It is the absence of any admissible MLB finals source that carries game ids. The finals in hand are owner relays keyed by team name, which identify a game to a reader and bind nothing. Fifteen games are sitting one source away from Tier 1 with no model change required.

Verdict

Zero evidence — not weak evidence. The refreshed model beats the constant prior by 0.002047 Brier while losing the straight-up record 2–2 to 3–1. The interval covers zero with room on both sides that exceeds the entire plausible effect.

What actually happened: the refreshed model was better on CON@IND (−0.205) and TOR@LV (−0.193), and worse on POR@ATL (+0.327) and WSH@LAS (+0.063). One game carries the whole deficit — POR@ATL, where it pushed p_home to 0.784 and Atlanta lost 101–83. The constant prior “won” the record by having no opinion at all: it forecast 0.5366 in every game.

Consequence: no promotion, no estimator update. The registered sample horizon is not met, and the interval does not exclude zero.

Against the standings fallback the refreshed model looks better — 0.061561 Brier — and that interval also covers zero (t = −0.9741, [−0.263, +0.140]). At n = 4 nothing separates from anything. That is the honest reading, and it is the reading regardless of which arm it flatters.

What the audit withdrew on its own initiative

mlb:2026-08-27 · downgraded

The 8/27 cohort had a binding proof publishing 7/7. It is withdrawn. The frozen board carries pseudo-pks 827000–827006, and the proof stamped result_date from a constant — which means DATE_COHERENCE_PASS was structurally incapable of failing.

This is the exact manufacture already withdrawn for 8/28 at commit 67104b9. Both instances are now withdrawn. A counter that cannot fail is not a check, and a passing counter is worth nothing until you have shown the arrangement under which it would have failed.

Honest limits

  • n = 4 is the entire inferential base. One bound cohort, four games, three arms. Any statement in this report stronger than “we cannot tell” is either descriptive or wrong.
  • The MLB paired intervals are the audit run's figures and were not re-derived here. They are descriptive and accrue nothing in either direction.
  • No CFB result in the window is admissible, so nothing is known about CFB model quality from this window — including the parts that looked good.
  • RJ-005 OFFICIAL_INJURY_AVAILABILITY stands at 0/17, egress-blocked. Lineup and rotation state is unverified for the WNBA arms, including the bound cohort.
  • The MLB pitcher-log corpus ends around 2026-07-19. MLB starter features are stale by construction, and no freshness contract repairs a corpus that stops.
  • MLB and WNBA cohorts are never pooled, and nothing in this report pools them.

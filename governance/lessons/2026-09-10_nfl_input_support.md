# Early-season NFL input support policy

This record follows the September 10 Rams–49ers diagnostic closeout. The frozen forecast and grades remain unchanged.

The verified defect is a gap between available information, applied model inputs, and the support communicated for the forecast. One loss does not prove that 69% was statistically miscalibrated. The game remains a forecast error; an input-support defect can coexist with it.

For future forecasts relying on prior-season ratings with material current-state information unapplied, disclose those omissions, retain DIAGNOSTIC support, and keep action authority OFF. Do not require all of travel differences, early-season timing, and captured-but-unapplied information to coincide before flagging a material gap. Capturing information alone does not reduce confidence, and different travel schedules alone do not prove disruption.

No dose=0.6 rule, automatic probability shrinkage, or variance inflation is authorized by this evidence. Numerical uncertainty changes require defined semantics and a preregistered prospective challenger, scored on calibration, proper scores, coverage and interval width. A wider interval is not automatically better calibrated.

Status: policy recorded on the PR18 branch; runtime enforcement NOT IMPLEMENTED; not merged to main; no v1.1 model released. This is a development requirement, not a claim that the runner already enforces it.

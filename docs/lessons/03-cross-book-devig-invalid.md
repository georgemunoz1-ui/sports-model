# Cross-book "best odds" pairs can never be de-vigged into a market probability.
Rule (owner, 8/3): each displayed best price may come from a different book; only a same-book two-sided pair is a valid de-vig unit. Best-odds boards support single-price EV screening only.
Why it matters: pairing best prices across books understates the true overround and manufactures both fake market probabilities and fake arbitrages.

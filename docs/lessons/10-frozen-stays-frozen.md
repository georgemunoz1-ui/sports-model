# Frozen rules are not loosened in-flight, even when the loosening looks reasonable (R3).
Rule: preregistered tokens mean what they say at execution time. Ambiguity is resolved conservatively (exclude and count) plus a prospective ruling request — never by reinterpretation mid-slate.
Example: V14 eligibility requires a capture that SAYS CONFIRMED; "both lineups posted" was excluded as AMBIGUOUS on 8/19, and the owner RATIFIED the exclusion (2026-08-20 ruling): expected/posted lineups never equal CONFIRMED — only an explicitly official confirmed-lineup source qualifies. Starvation is acceptable; contamination is not.

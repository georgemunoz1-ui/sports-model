Two edges, both shown, because they answer different questions. Edge vs break-even is what EV is a function of — the break-even is the probability the price demands, vig included. Edge vs no-vig is disagreement with the market once the hold is removed, and it is always the larger. Quoting the larger one as “the edge” is how a disagreement becomes an edge in a summary.

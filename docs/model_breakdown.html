Basketball: possessions and efficiency

Estimated team possessions = FGA - offensive rebounds + turnovers + 0.44 * FTA
Game possession estimate   = average of the two team estimates
Offensive rating           = 100 * points / possessions
Defensive rating           = 100 * points allowed / possessions
Expected score template    = predicted possessions * matchup points per possession

These are estimator/expectation definitions, not proof of forecast accuracy. The free-throw coefficient is an approximation. Pace, opponent effects, uncertainty, overtime, personnel and calibration must be fitted and evaluated under the candidate's actual contract. The possession-efficiency experiment exists; a newly repaired superior formula is not claimed. Sport-specific next experiments.
